The Restorative Justice Hub at the University of California, Berkeley is a place to learn how communities respond to harm, build relationships, and pursue accountability and repair.

What the Hub brings together

Restorative practices make room for people to describe how they were affected, listen to one another, and decide how they want to participate. Explore the Hub’s learning guides and project materials to see how those ideas inform restorative practice.

The Hub and the former Center

The former Berkeley Restorative Justice Center is part of the history of restorative justice at Berkeley. The historical archive covers the Center’s earlier work. Find information on current activities under Offerings.
