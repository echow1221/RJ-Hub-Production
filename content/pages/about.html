The Restorative Justice Hub at the University of California, Berkeley brings restorative justice into view as a way to learn about harm, accountability, and repair.

Learning through dialogue

A restorative approach starts with people’s experiences. It asks who was affected, what they need, and who can take responsibility for making things right. The circle, a recurring image in the Hub’s identity, reflects the space for listening and participation that can make these conversations possible.

The former Center and the present Hub

The Restorative Justice Center and the Restorative Justice Hub are distinct. The Center’s historical programs and projects belong to its own record. Descriptions of that earlier work should be read in their original context and time period.
