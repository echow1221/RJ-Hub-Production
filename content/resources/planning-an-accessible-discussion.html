Use this checklist to think through accessibility before a group discussion. Ask participants what helps them take part, and give people time to respond.

Before the discussion

  • Share the topic, format, and materials ahead of time.
  • Check that people can reach the space and move around it comfortably.
  • Offer materials in a format participants can use with assistive technology.

During the discussion

  • Explain how to take a turn, pass, or ask for a break.
  • Offer more than one way to contribute where practical.
  • Summarize next steps in plain language and provide a way to review them later.
