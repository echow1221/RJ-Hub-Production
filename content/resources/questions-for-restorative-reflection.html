These prompts introduce questions that can guide reflection after harm. Use them for study or discussion in a setting where people can choose how much to share.

Understand what happened

  • How do people describe the situation from their own perspectives?
  • Who was affected, and what impacts have they identified?
  • What information or support would help people participate by choice?

Consider repair

  • What would help address the harm now?
  • Who is able to take responsibility for each next step?
  • How can people check whether the agreed steps have been carried out?
